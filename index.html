Earning Scenarios

Referred Buyer (You Refer for Feedback)
70%
You referred a buyer who submits feedback. You earn 70% of the commission the vendor pays to this marketplace for that feedback.
Example: Vendor commission on your referred buyer's feedback $10 → You earn $7.00
Referred Vendor (Vendor You Referred)
30%
You referred a vendor. When anyone submits feedback on that vendor, you earn 30% of the commission the vendor pays to this marketplace.
Example: Vendor commission $10 for feedback → You earn $3.00
Both Referred (Vendor & Feedback Submitter)
30-60%
You referred a vendor and a friend who submits feedback. You earn 30% as vendor referrer and 30% as buyer referrer (total 60% split from commission).
Example: Vendor commission $10 for feedback → You earn $3.00 (vendor) + $3.00 (buyer) = $6.00
Your Feedback Submission
Variable
You submit feedback on a product. You earn a per-feedback commission agreed between you and the vendor (default minimum $5).
Example: Vendor's per-feedback rate $7 → You earn $7.00 per feedback
